# Report summary-figure & benchmark audit

Read-only audit (Part 1), written before any code change.

## 1. Which "main summary" figures are confusing/redundant?
`bulk_main_summary_figure` and `spatial_main_summary_figure` (generated by
`plotting/summary_figures.py`, called in `07_generate_reports.py`
`generate_bulk_outputs`/`generate_spatial_outputs`). They are multi-panel
composite "diagnostic dumps" (composition + reference family counts + top pairs
in one crowded figure) rather than a single clear user-facing plot.

## 2. Do they duplicate better plots?
**Yes.** Their panels duplicate dedicated, clearer figures already in the
report: `bulk_composition_clustered_barplot`, `bulk_composition_heatmap`,
`reference_broad_family_composition`, `top_confusable_pairs` (bulk) and
`spatial_mean_composition_barplot`, `spatial_dominant_cell_type_map`,
`spatial_abundance_maps`, `spatial_morans_i_barplot` (spatial).

## 3. Source data & captions?
They have a `.data.tsv` and (after the prior caption pass) a non-generic
title-derived caption, but the caption cannot meaningfully describe a composite
multi-panel figure, and `_figure_cards` sorts alphabetically so the composite
sits **interleaved among the primary figures** (bulk: 3rd; spatial: 8th).

## 4. Disposition
**Move to a collapsible "Technical composite diagnostics" appendix** at the end
of each modality section, labelled as a technical summary, and **exclude from
the primary figure grid**. (Removal is also acceptable; moving preserves the
artifact + its source data without crowding the primary view.)

## 5. Does the benchmark mix bulk and spatial metrics?
**Partly.** The single "9. Benchmark comparison" section renders
`benchmark_method_status_summary`, `bulk_accuracy_leaderboard`,
`runtime_comparison`, `composite_scorecard` together. The accuracy leaderboard
already excludes spatial methods (prior fix), but **method status, runtime, and
the scorecard mix both modalities in one section**, and there is **no separate
spatial benchmark section** with concordance/structure metrics.

## 6. Are bulk/spatial methods ranked together?
Not in the accuracy leaderboard (bulk-only) and not in the composite (ranked
per-modality, prior fix). But they are **presented together** in one section,
which reads as a shared comparison; there is no dedicated spatial-structure view.

## 7. Are spatial results ever shown as accuracy without ground truth?
**No** — the bulk accuracy leaderboard excludes spatial, and the composite is
labelled "NOT objective accuracy". The gap is the **absence** of a proper
spatial benchmark (concordance/structure), not a false accuracy claim.

## 8. What needs changing
- **Plotting:** new `plotting/bulk_benchmark_plots.py` and
  `plotting/spatial_benchmark_plots.py` (refactor/extend the existing
  `benchmark_report_plots.py`).
- **Report (`07_generate_reports.py`):**
  - `_figure_cards`: support excluding figures from the primary grid; render
    `*_main_summary_figure` in a collapsible "Technical composite diagnostics"
    appendix in the bulk/spatial sections.
  - Split section 9 into **9 Bulk benchmark** (accuracy, ground-truth statement),
    **10 Spatial benchmark** (concordance/structure, explicit no-ground-truth
    statement), **11 Benchmark scorecards & method status**; renumber Warnings→12,
    Methods→13, Outputs→14; update sidebar nav.
  - Manifest sections `bulk_benchmark` / `spatial_benchmark` /
    `benchmark_scorecard`.
  - Specific captions stating metric type (accuracy / concordance / structure /
    runtime / scorecard).
- **generate_benchmark_figures:** write bulk vs spatial vs scorecard figures into
  separate `benchmark/{bulk,spatial,scorecard}/figures` dirs.

## Constraints
No core-algorithm/prediction change; do not overclaim spatial accuracy; do not
hide warnings; nothing committed.
